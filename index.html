Rising floor
A partial row shoves in from below and everything moves up. The gap between rises shrinks with every level.

Combo window
Each merge opens a 2.6 s window. Merge again inside it and the multiplier climbs, up to 9×.
Charge & vent
Merging charges the meter. Full, it blows out the bottom row — space, not time. One vent per rise.
